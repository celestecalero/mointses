Gear Care & Repair

The goal of Endless Promise® is to keep our gear out of the landfill.

At Mointses, we understand that nature is our one true home. That is why we design outdoor gear and apparel not only to withstand the most extreme weather conditions but also to protect the ecosystems we love to explore.

Our Endless Promise initiative stems from the urgent need to change how we consume and produce within the textile industry. We do not want our garments ending up in landfills after just a few seasons of use.

Every jacket, tent, and backpack we manufacture is conceived based on circular economy principles. We use high-tech recycled materials that ensure waterproofing, warmth, and breathability without compromising mountain performance.

But sustainability goes beyond materials. We actively encourage the repair of our gear. We believe a patched garment tells a story of adventure, effort, and respect for the planet's resources.

Our design team works daily with athletes and mountain guides to put every prototype to the test. This constant feedback ensures that every seam is perfectly placed and serves a vital function.

By choosing Mointses, you are not just preparing for your next summit; you are joining a global community of adventurers committed to leaving the trails better than we found them. Adventure calls—let us answer responsibly.
